Ad Pulse Lab — self test

You will watch 3 short coffee ads (about 60 s each) while the camera measures breathing and pulse on this PC.

No video and no face data are saved. Only breathing and pulse numbers, your answers, and quality warnings are written to a local log file.

  • Turn on the room light in front of you. Avoid a window or lamp behind you.
  • Put the camera on the desk so your face and upper chest are visible.
  • Do not talk, chew gum, or move much while measuring. The whole session takes about 8 minutes.
