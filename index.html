Ad Pulse Lab

You will watch 3 short coffee ads (60–80 s each) while the camera measures breathing and pulse on this PC.

No video and no face data are saved. At the end you review your numbers and decide whether to save a result file.

  • Turn on the room light in front of you. Avoid a window or lamp behind you.
  • Put the camera on the desk so your face and upper chest are visible.
  • Do not talk, chew gum, or move much while measuring — especially during the gray rest screen, which is the reference for everything else. The whole session takes about 9 minutes.

Before we start

Please read this, then tick the boxes you agree to. You can stop at any time by closing the window.

What happens

  • Your webcam is used to measure your breathing and pulse while you watch 3 short coffee ads (about 9 minutes in total).
  • The measurement runs on this PC (Presage SmartSpectra SDK). The camera video and your face are never saved and never sent anywhere.
  • The SDK connects to Presage only to check the access key. SDK usage statistics (telemetry) are turned off. No AI service is used.
  • After each ad you answer two questions yourself: Would you buy it? and Did you like the ad? The app does not guess your feelings.
  • This is not a medical test and the numbers are not health advice.

Your results

  • At the end you see your own numbers first. Only if you press Approve and save is a result file created. Delete removes this session's data from this PC.
  • The file contains: a random ID (no name), your answers, and breathing and pulse numbers. No video, no face data.
  • You send the file yourself to the organizer. You can ask the organizer to delete it at any time; the organizer deletes all files after the contest results (December 2026).
